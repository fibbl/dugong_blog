Topic: the customs data that went from optional to load-bearing in under a year, the HS code and
country of origin fields most catalogs never filled in because de minimis made blanks free. The
post covers the regulatory year that ended that (Executive Order 14324 signed July 30 2025,
effective August 29 2025 for every country and value; 20+ national postal operators pausing
US-bound parcels the same week, Japan Post the 25th, Australia Post the 26th, Royal Mail and
much of Europe from the 27th; the $80/$160/$200 postal flat-fee window closing end of February
2026; the EU removing its 150 euro exemption July 1 2026 and charging a flat 3 euros per six
digit tariff subheading until July 2028, which makes the HS code the unit the bill is counted
in; the November 1 2026 EU three-identifier requirement with Shopify holding native fields for
only two); the platform mechanics verified against the help center (both fields per variant with
no product-level inheritance; three ways in: product page search, bulk editor, the duties CSV
under Settings > Taxes and duties whose export doubles as the only coverage report and only runs
when you fetch it; store-level default for country of origin since May 2025 and no equivalent
for HS codes; the estimation cascade, missing HS falls back to description and product category,
missing those the calculation still runs and is "likely to be inaccurate"; the caution that
inaccurate customs data "might result in additional duties being charged to the customer upon
delivery"; Managed Markets assigning codes that are not guaranteed to match yours; the duty
calculation fee of 0.85%/1.5%, temporarily 0.5% since February 2025, charged even when the
calculation ran on blanks); the community record (June 25 2026 feature request: 17 color
variants of one candle, the same code typed 17 times; the July 27 reply doing the arithmetic,
three variants across 400 products is 1,200 fields, and explaining why a blind product-level
default would be wrong, leather and canvas land on different headings; the August 31 2025
tariff-calculation thread from a merchant with 70% of their business in the US, support's
written concession that the calculator was using the commercial method against a postal
carrier, no timeline, "you are not the only merchant"; the "Shopify charging 100 percent
duties" thread at 20 replies and 1,253 views still active August 4 2026, one merchant measuring
95-100% of price where 3.5-5% was owed); the enforcement turn (DOJ-DHS trade fraud task force,
whistleblower incentives for reporting intentional down-coding); and the playbook (audit
coverage per variant including weights, classify with AI proposals plus confidence and human
review of the tail, batch high-confidence into the bulk editor with an audit trail, guard every
newly published product the same hour, hold international orders carrying a codeless line item
before the label prints, compare checkout duty against carrier invoices as a misclassification
report, report coverage monthly).
