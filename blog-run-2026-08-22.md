**Dispatch Nº 112 - "The stock count that changes overnight"**
`how-to-stop-your-shopify-inventory-changing-by-itself.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-22 · ~2,800 words · 11 min

Topic: Shopify inventory that changes with no sale, return, or staff edit behind it. Sold out
products refill themselves, full products zero out at night, and the adjustment history can
show nothing at all. The post covers the community record (the May 2022 "inventory count
increasing unexpectedly on its own" thread, 13 replies, active through Feb 2025: counts higher
than any quantity ever ordered; the Jan 2023 toy shop whose sold-out Christmas ranges came
back at their original numbers, self-diagnosed a day later as the Faire purchasing connection
writing retail stock, and the same failure again for another merchant in Jan 2025 with 40
phantom products after a week of year-end counting; "items with 1 unit shows more than 40";
"ongoing for 2 years now and support are unable to assist", at least once a month; the Aug
2023 merchant whose low-stock Flow alert caught his digital products resetting to zero at
9:20pm five nights running, 1000 set back, zero again; the Sep 2023 discovery that duplicating
a product doubled its inventory, "Shopify's response just blames the user"; the Dec 2024
thrift shop turning away sales; the settled workaround "show one less inventory than we
actually have"; the Feb 2025 five-year merchant threatening to leave; and the May 2023 URGENT
thread where the majority of a catalog reset to zero overnight with no inventory apps and an
adjustment history showing no evidence, answered ten months later only by "Was this resolved?
Having the same issue today and no one can help"); the Shopify staff position from June 2023
("Inventory number should not be changed without a user or an app making those changes");
why counts move (channels and purchasing connections, dropship resyncs, duplication doubling,
CSV overwrites, POS taps, second-store syncs, bundle math, Available-vs-On-hand API writes);
the admin's limits from the help docs (adjustment history per variant only, 180-day window,
"Data correction" defined as "an error correction that was made automatically", Created by
column); the stock take as the right idea in the wrong tool; and the playbook (baseline each
SKU's normal movement and permitted writers, watch every adjustment, match each change to its
story, flag orphans in minutes with evidence, name the culprit from Created by plus pattern,
keep a ledger past 180 days, report weekly), with the honest-limits paragraph stating that no
automation stops a misbehaving integration from writing or restores an unwatched overwrite;
what changes is discovery time and attribution.
