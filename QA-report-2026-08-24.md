## Defect 2 (caught and fixed within this run): converter swallowed a paragraph

While generating the llms-full.txt entry, this run's own HTML-to-text converter reproduced
the exact failure class documented on 08-23: the paragraph immediately following the
`<pre id="playbook">` block ("Step two is the missing column in the admin: shelf age...")
was swallowed. The fence itself survived. Caught by the round-trip verification, and the
paragraph was restored in place after the fence. Post-repair, the whitespace-normalized
word round-trip against the source page shows no missing content (remaining diffs are
punctuation tokenization and the byline chrome that entries exclude by convention). This
confirms the standing recommendation: any converter that touches `<pre>` needs its
following-sibling handling tested, and round-trip verification should stay mandatory.
