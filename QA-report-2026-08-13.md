## Defect 1: Nº 107 postal-pause dates (content accuracy)

The body paragraph on the August 2025 postal suspensions read "Japan Post on the 25th,
Australia Post on the 26th, Royal Mail and much of Europe from the 27th, paused US-bound
parcels entirely." Checked against coverage this run: Japan Post **announced** on the 25th
and its suspension took effect the 27th; Royal Mail withdrew its USA export services on
Tuesday the **26th** (resuming the 28th on PDDP); most European operators (DHL/Deutsche Post,
PostNord, bpost, La Poste) stopped in the days **before** the 27th; and several suspensions
were partial rather than total, so "entirely" overclaimed. Rewrote the one sentence to
"Japan Post announcing on the 25th, Australia Post and Royal Mail stopping on the 26th, much
of Europe in the same few days, paused US-bound parcels in whole or in part." No other block
carries per-operator dates: the NOTE box, og/JSON-LD descriptions, feed, and llms.txt all say
only "the same week," which remains true. dateModified 2026-08-13 already covers a same-day
edit; no sitemap/feed ripple. Re-verified after the edit: tag balance clean, zero raw em/en
dashes outside CSS comments, `<style>` still byte-identical (md5) to the standard sheet,
