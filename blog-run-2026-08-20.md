cancel; the March 2022 "orders not loading" thread with everything "connected correctly",
still drawing replies May 2025; the sibling connection thread at 8/677 active May 2025; the
Oct 2025 zero-reply thread where a merchant self-published a YouTube workaround after
discovering marked-fulfilled orders are silently blocked from importing); why the seam leaks
(voided/repaid auths, marked-fulfilled skips, manual and draft orders, holds, archived orders,
ShipStation filters/tags/second stores, wrong status); the cost ledger (nine silent days,
WISMO tickets, apology shipping, chargebacks, and the "known unknowns" problem: you only count
the ones you catch); the manual diff as the right idea in the wrong tool ("a nightmare in busy
seasons where we'll have thousands of orders per day"); and the playbook (baseline normal sync
lag, reconcile every paid open order on a clock across all statuses, read payment-timeline
signatures, chase gaps with probable cause attached, watch the reverse path for tracking that
never lands back in Shopify, morning digest, weekly leak report), with the honest-limits
paragraph stating that no automation patches the export pipeline itself; what changes is
