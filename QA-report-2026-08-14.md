## Findings triaged as non-defects

- **Raw em/en dash characters in ~34 older files.** Older posts carry literal dash characters
  in title-tag suffixes, RSS link titles, ticker chrome, and CSS comments, while newer posts
  encode the same chrome as `&mdash;` entities. Both render identically in every browser;
  encoding style is the only difference, there is no reader-visible inconsistency, and body
  prose is clean in all 60 posts. Left as is.
- **"Cant run ads due to out of stock item."** in the paused-ads post is a verbatim quoted
  merchant forum thread title, inside quotation marks, and is deliberate. Prior QA runs
  reached the same ruling.
- **Scanner artifacts, all false positives:** "cant" hits inside the word "applicant is";
  "usefull" inside "usefully"; "wich" inside the "TurkeySandwich" case-sensitivity example;
  repeated-word candidates ("Shopify Shopify", "writing Writing", "returns returns",
  "cart cart") are all title/dek or topic-chip adjacency artifacts of tag-stripped text
  extraction, with correct markup between them in the rendered page.
- **The rejected draft `how-to-send-payment-reminders-for-unpaid-draft-orders-on-shopify.html`
  lacks the mobile `pre-wrap` rule.** It is one of the two noindexed, unlinked, unpublished
  drafts and stays untouched per the standing convention; if it is ever published it should
  first be brought onto the standard sheet.
- `.fuse_hidden*` files at the folder root remain filesystem artifacts of the mounted volume,
  safe to ignore, as noted in previous reports.
