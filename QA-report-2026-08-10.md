## Fix applied

### feed.xml: wrong weekday name on the two May 28 pubDates

The two oldest feed items — the lead essay ("The best Shopify automations to set up",
09:00:00) and the pinned Issue 47 essay ("The death of the drag-and-drop builder", 08:00:00) —
both stated `Wed, 28 May 2026`. May 28, 2026 was a **Thursday**; both posts' own pages and
JSON-LD correctly say 2026-05-28, so only the RFC 822 day-name in the feed was wrong. RSS
readers and validators cross-check the weekday against the date, and strict parsers flag the
mismatch. Changed `Wed,` to `Thu,` on both lines; nothing else in the file touched. All 57
items plus lastBuildDate now carry weekday names that match their dates (verified by
recomputing the weekday for every date in the file). This had been present since the feed's
creation; prior sweeps validated date parsing and future-dating but never weekday/date
agreement — that check is now part of the suite.
