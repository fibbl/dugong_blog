## New post

**Dispatch Nº 106 - "The misprice that sells all night"**
`how-to-catch-pricing-errors-on-shopify-before-orders-flood-in.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-12 · 2,700 words · 10 min

Topic: the pricing error that publishes, the wrong price that goes live the instant it saves and
sells at full checkout speed with no alarm anywhere. The post covers the five unguarded doors into
the price field (admin edits, the bulk editor, CSV imports with blank-cell overwrites and the
regional-separator multiplier, and every app with write scope: supplier syncs, repricers, currency
tools, sale schedulers; no draft state, no review queue, price is production); the three structural
gaps verified against the platform (Flow's product trigger list has eight triggers including
variant inventory quantity changed and nothing for price, so a one-unit stock move fires an event
while a hundred-dollar price move fires nothing; the products update webhook reports neither the
changed field nor the previous value, so any watcher must store and diff; no price history, no
undo, no version log, and the activity log covers logins and app installs, not product fields);
the community record asking for exactly this guardrail; the famous incidents that set the clock
(6pm.com capped at $49.95 from midnight to 6 am in May 2010, over $1.6M honored in full; the
RepricerExpress fault of December 2014 that sent listings from roughly 2,000 Amazon UK sellers to
1p for about an hour, 675 costumes at 1p for one seller at £15,000 to £20,000 expected loss, 1,600
orders in sixty minutes for another, and Amazon's line that shipped orders could not be canceled
or reimbursed); the legal shape phrased softly (displayed price as invitation to treat, terms
reserving cancellation of obvious errors, the decision existing only while orders are unshipped);
and the playbook (snapshot the catalog, watch every write, judge the change by store rules,
quarantine first, circuit-break the orders with fulfillment holds, decide honor-or-cancel by
policy, report the writes monthly).
