**Dispatch Nº 105 - "The package that stops moving"**
`how-to-catch-shopify-orders-stuck-in-transit.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-12 · 2,600 words · 10 min

Topic: the mid-transit stall, the shipped order whose tracking goes quiet, positioned as the
middle silence of shipping between two existing dispatches (the pre-ship delay nobody
announces, Nº for shipping delays, and the delivered-but-not-received claim). The post covers
why Shopify is structurally blind to it (every shipping signal fires on an event arriving:
status chips advance on carrier scans, all shipping emails are milestone-triggered, the dev
docs' 11 fulfillment event statuses even include a delayed slot that nothing in the admin acts
on; shipment status advances only for carriers Shopify recognizes, so unrecognized carriers
sit on Tracking added for the whole journey; no tracking number leaves the order On its way
until manually marked delivered); the community record asking for exactly this alert (May 2024
Shopify Plus thread: packages travel two weeks with no update and remain on Tracking added,
merchant wants to tag and alert after X days "so we can take actions before customers
complaining"; the Flow PM reply points at order / fulfillment orders / fulfillments /
displayStatus and concedes duration-in-state needs storing state yourself or Wait steps;
Feb 2025 thread asks for 7-days-in-transit emails; Dec 2025 thread sits at zero replies); the
three clocks that make a stall urgent (normal scan gaps of 24-72 hours domestic and longer on
consolidator/cross-border lanes; the first-mile species where no acceptance scan means the box
never left your building; Descartes' 8,000-consumer survey, 67 percent hit a delivery problem
in a quarter and 63 percent of those punished the brand; carrier windows all measured from the
ship date: USPS Missing Mail search at 7 days from mailing, UPS claims open 24 hours after the
scheduled delivery date with notice inside roughly 60 days, FedEx on the same order); and the
playbook (learn the lane, watch the first mile, flag the stall at the lane's own threshold,
tell the customer first, open the trace on the carrier's clock, decide the save by policy,
report the lanes monthly).
