**Dispatch Nº 104 - "The gift card that never arrives"**
`how-to-automate-gift-card-delivery-and-reminders-on-shopify.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-10 · 2,600 words · 10 min

Topic: the full gift card lifecycle after checkout, the two silences nobody staffs. The post
covers the delivery half (the notification email gated by fulfillment, auto-fulfilled on
payment by default but silently skipped on medium and high risk orders per the help docs'
verbatim note; the recipient form as a theme feature, introduced in v9.0.0 of the free OS 2.0
themes, Dawn and the Horizon family, absent on vintage themes, off until the Show recipient
information form checkbox is found in the Buy buttons block; the form validating only through
Add to Cart so accelerated checkout buttons drop recipient details, which is the opening
scene; Send on scheduling capped at 90 days out with sends going 4:00 am to 10:00 am in the
purchasing customer's timezone, not the recipient's; the two notification templates, New gift
card and Gift card receipt, and how customizations strip recipient fields and the Apple
Wallet button); the sent-is-not-delivered gap (no delivered, bounced, or spam state anywhere;
typo'd recipient emails reporting success; the admin showing only the last four characters of
any code after creation, so Resend gift card is the only repair and nothing says which cards
need it); the unspent-balance half (no balance reminders or expiry warnings natively; expiry
under Settings > Payments defaulting to never, five-year default term where set, the CARD Act
2009 federal floor, state bans and escheatment hedged appropriately; Flow having no gift card
trigger of any kind while the Get gift card data action can query 100 cards on a schedule, so
the raw material for a reminder program exists and the program does not); and the playbook
(watch the send, verify the landing, honor the date on the recipient's clock, wake the
balance at 30/90 days, warn before the cliff at 60/14 days, rescue the lost card same hour,
report liability monthly).
